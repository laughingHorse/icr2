Race Setup

The player handling is locked to the Detroit reference baseline. These controls change race resources and presentation, not the base steering/grip model.

Driving & Race Settings

Steering and grip are the locked Phase 4.1 model in both trims. High-Speed changes only drag, gearing and the steering lock above Classic's 141 mph top speed. AI pace and straight-line cap are remembered separately for each trim (High-Speed defaults: 100% pace, cap at the 6th-gear top speed).
